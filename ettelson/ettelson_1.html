BODY FOUND AFTER DAYS
IN PARKED CAR

By ERIN CALABRESE and JOE McGURK

May 13, 2006 -- A corpse rotting in the backseat of a car in Brooklyn was only discovered after a passer-by sensed a foul odor wafting from the vehicle, which had numerous parking tickets.

The badly decomposed body was discovered under blankets in the rear of a burgundy Toyota Camry that was parked near Veronica Place and Beverley Road in Flatbush at 9 p.m.

The car had parking tickets and a sanitation violation sticker on the window.

"You mean to tell me they put tickets on there and they didn't smell it?" said neighbor Talisa Wright, 24, who walked by the car for at least three days. "We didn't even know there was a dead body in it. It just smelled like a dead mouse or a rat or something."

A plastic bag was tied around the head of the unidentified victim, cops said. Detectives also removed several bottles of pills.

The death appeared to be a suicide, cops said.

The Toyota is registered to an Andrew Ettelson, 40, of Forest Hills, Queens. He could not be reached last night.
