BODY IN CAR WAS MAN DRIVEN TO SUICIDE BY LONELINESS

By JOHN DOYLE and HASANI GITTENS

May 14, 2006 -- The man whose body was found decomposing in the back of a parking-ticket-covered car in Brooklyn on Friday was apparently desperately seeking human contact, The Post has learned.

Police said that Andrew Ettelson, 40, of Forest Hills, Queens, had a history of mental illness and depression before he apparently suffocated himself with a plastic bag in the back of his car in Flatbush.

All signs point to suicide, especially since numerous pills were also found in the car, police sources said, but autopsy results are pending.

Part of his problems came when he broke up with this girlfriend about a month ago, after a long, loud argument, said a roommate at the woman's house.

Ettelson, a Class of 1971 Harvard alum, is a member of myspace.com, friendster.com and numerous "social networking" groups on meetup.com - which takes Internet connectivity to the next level: outside, into the real world.

Ettelson also joined a group called Drinking Liberally which connects like-minded liberals with libations after a hard day at work.

One person who met Ettelson at one of the Drinking Liberally meet-ups at Rudy's Bar and Grill in Hell's Kitchen said he was "really on edge" and made her nervous.

Ettelson's body was found in the back of his Toyota Camry on Veronica Place and Beverly Road in Brooklyn at about 9 p.m. Friday.
